I combine theory-driven perspectives with hands-on work using frameworks such as PyTorch, Hugging Face (Transformers/Diffusers), TRL, Accelerate, DeepSpeed, and PEFT/LoRA. My background includes strong foundations in information theory, [high dimensional statistics](../../_technical_content/Solutions_to_HDS_Martin_Wainwright.pdf), and RL, supported by a deep interest in mathematics and principled model development. Before joining my PhD, I earned an MTech in Signal Processing from the [Indian Institute of Science](https://iisc.ac.in/), Bengaluru, where I worked on probabilistic methods with [Dr. Parimal Parag](https://ece.iisc.ac.in/~parimal/). Across my projects, I’m driven by theory-guided insights that lead to practical, reliable, and scalable AI systems.
